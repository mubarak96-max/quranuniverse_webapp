Surah Yasin (also spelled Yaseen, Yassin or Ya-Sin) is the 36th surah of the Quran, a chapter of 83 verses about God's messengers, His signs in nature, and the certainty of resurrection. It is famously called "the heart of the Quran", although many hadith scholars consider the narration behind that title weak.

Muslims around the world recite Surah Yasin often, including in times of hardship and near those who are dying. This guide explains what the surah actually says, verse by verse, and separates the authentic reports about its benefits from the weaker ones.

## Key takeaways

- Surah Yasin is the 36th surah of the Quran, has 83 verses, and was revealed in Mecca before the Prophet's migration to Madinah.
- It is named after its two opening Arabic letters, Ya and Sin, whose exact meaning most scholars say is known only to Allah.
- Its main themes are the truth of the Prophet's message, a town that rejected its messengers, signs of God in creation, and life after death.
- The hadith calling Surah Yasin "the heart of the Quran" is recorded in Jami at-Tirmidhi but is graded weak by many hadith scholars.
- The general reward for reciting the Quran, ten good deeds for every letter, is authentic and applies fully to Surah Yasin.

## What is Surah Yasin?

[Surah Yasin](/surah/ya-sin) is a Meccan surah, revealed during the years when the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) called the people of Mecca to worship one God and many rejected him. Its verses are short, rhythmic and powerful, which makes it one of the most memorized surahs of the Quran. It begins in the 22nd juz and ends in the 23rd.

The surah opens with two letters, Ya and Sin. Twenty-nine surahs begin with such "disconnected letters" (*al-huruf al-muqatta'at*). Some early commentators suggested Ya-Sin means "O human" or is a name of the Prophet, but most scholars hold that the true meaning is known only to Allah. Right after the letters comes an oath by the Quran itself.

> By the wise Qur'an. Indeed you, [O Muhammad], are from among the messengers, On a straight path.
>
> — [Quran 36:2–4](/surah/ya-sin/2)

"Allah" here is simply the Arabic word for God, used by Arab Christians as well as Muslims.

## Surah Yasin in English: meaning and main themes

Surah Yasin moves through four connected themes. Reading it with these in mind makes the surah much easier to follow in English.

### 1. The Prophet's mission and the record of deeds (verses 1–12)

The opening confirms that Muhammad is a genuine messenger and that the Quran is a revelation "of the Exalted in Might, the Merciful." It then reminds the reader that every action, and even its lasting effects, is recorded.

> Indeed, it is We who bring the dead to life and record what they have put forth and what they left behind, and all things We have enumerated in a clear register.
>
> — [Quran 36:12](/surah/ya-sin/12)

Scholars explain "what they left behind" as the good or harm a person leaves in the world, such as knowledge they taught, charity that keeps benefiting others, or a bad example that others follow.

### 2. The people of the town (verses 13–32)

God tells the story of a town that received two messengers, then a third. The people rejected and threatened them. Then a man came running from the far end of the town to defend the messengers. Classical commentators, as recorded by Ibn Kathir, named him Habib, though the Quran does not.

> And there came from the farthest end of the city a man, running. He said, "O my people, follow the messengers. Follow those who do not ask of you [any] payment, and they are [rightly] guided.
>
> — [Quran 36:20–21](/surah/ya-sin/20)

His people killed him, according to the commentators, yet his only concern after death was for them.

> It was said, "Enter Paradise." He said, "I wish my people could know Of how my Lord has forgiven me and placed me among the honored."
>
> — [Quran 36:26–27](/surah/ya-sin/26)

The lesson is courage in standing up for truth, and sincere care even for those who harm you.

### 3. Signs of God in creation (verses 33–44)

The surah then turns to nature: dead earth brought back to life by rain, gardens and springs, night and day, the sun and the moon. Each is presented as a sign pointing to the Creator, and as a reason for gratitude.

> It is not allowable for the sun to reach the moon, nor does the night overtake the day, but each, in an orbit, is swimming.
>
> — [Quran 36:40](/surah/ya-sin/40)

### 4. Resurrection, judgment and "Be, and it is" (verses 45–83)

The second half describes the Day of Resurrection: the blowing of the Horn, people rising from their graves, and every soul judged fairly. The people of Paradise are greeted with a word of peace "from a Merciful Lord" ([36:58](/surah/ya-sin/58)).

The surah closes by answering the doubt at the heart of disbelief in the afterlife.

> And he presents for Us an example and forgets his [own] creation. He says, "Who will give life to bones while they are disintegrated?" Say, "He will give them life who produced them the first time; and He is, of all creation, Knowing."
>
> — [Quran 36:78–79](/surah/ya-sin/78)

> His command is only when He intends a thing that He says to it, "Be," and it is.
>
> — [Quran 36:82](/surah/ya-sin/82)

The logic is simple and powerful: the One who created us the first time can easily create us again.

## Surah Yasin benefits: what is authentic?

Many benefits of Surah Yasin circulate online and in pamphlets. Because this is a question of what the Prophet actually said, it helps to know how scholars grade each report.

### "The heart of the Quran"

Jami at-Tirmidhi records a narration that everything has a heart, and the heart of the Quran is Yasin, and that reading it once earns the reward of reading the Quran ten times. Many hadith scholars have graded this narration weak (*da'if*), and some considered it even weaker. So it should not be presented as a confirmed saying of the Prophet.

Even so, many scholars note that the surah does gather the Quran's core message in a short space: the oneness of God, the truth of prophethood, and the resurrection. That is a fair way to understand why the title became so popular.

### Reading Yasin at night

Ibn Hibban and others report that whoever recites Yasin at night seeking the pleasure of Allah will be forgiven. Scholars differ on the strength of this narration, with some accepting it and others considering it weak.

### What is certain

The general virtue of reciting the Quran is beyond doubt. Jami at-Tirmidhi records an authentic hadith that whoever reads one letter of the Book of Allah earns a good deed, and each good deed is multiplied by ten. Surah Yasin is part of that Book, so its recitation is rewarded, and its reminders about death and the afterlife soften the heart.

## Surah Yasin for the dead and dying

A hadith in Sunan Abi Dawud and Sunan Ibn Majah says, "Recite Yasin over your dying." Several hadith scholars, including ad-Daraqutni, considered its chain weak, while Ibn Hibban accepted it. Many scholars explain that "your dying" refers to people who are near death rather than those already buried.

Despite the weakness of the chain, many scholars across the schools of law still regard reciting Yasin near a dying person as a good practice. Its themes of mercy, resurrection and a believer told "Enter Paradise" are seen as a comfort at that moment. Reciting the Quran on behalf of someone who has already died, at a grave or in a gathering, is a matter on which scholars hold different views.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Surah Yasin called the heart of the Quran?

The title comes from a narration in Jami at-Tirmidhi stating that everything has a heart and the heart of the Quran is Yasin. Many hadith scholars grade this narration weak, so it is not confirmed as the Prophet's words. Scholars note, however, that the surah concisely gathers the Quran's central themes of God's oneness, prophethood and resurrection.

### What are the benefits of Surah Yasin?

The certain benefit is the reward for reciting the Quran, which an authentic hadith in Jami at-Tirmidhi describes as ten good deeds for every letter. Specific claims, such as Yasin equalling ten readings of the Quran, come from narrations that many scholars consider weak. The surah's reminders about death, judgment and God's mercy are themselves a major spiritual benefit.

### How many verses are in Surah Yasin?

Surah Yasin has 83 verses. It is the 36th surah of the Quran, was revealed in Mecca, and spans the 22nd and 23rd juz (parts) of the Quran.

### Is it Surah Yasin, Yaseen or Ya-Sin?

All of these spellings refer to the same surah. The name is made of two Arabic letters, Ya and Sin, and English writers spell it in different ways, including Yasin, Yaseen, Yassin and Ya-Sin. The meaning of the two letters is, according to most scholars, known only to Allah.

### Can Surah Yasin be read for the dead?

The hadith "Recite Yasin over your dying" is recorded in Sunan Abi Dawud but is graded weak by many scholars, who also explain it as referring to people close to death. Many scholars still consider reciting Yasin near the dying a good practice. Reciting it for someone who has already died is a question on which scholars differ.

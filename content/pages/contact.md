## Reporting an error

Accuracy matters to us more than anything else on this site. If you think a verse card, translation, transliteration or guide contains a mistake, please email us with:

- The address of the page, or the surah and verse number (for example 2:255)
- What you think is wrong
- A source, if you have one, such as a tafsir or a scholar's explanation

We review every report and update the page when a correction is needed.

## Other messages

You can also write to us about:

- Suggestions for new guides or features
- Questions about how the site works
- Permission requests and copyright questions
- Privacy requests about any information you have sent us (see our [privacy policy](/privacy))

## Religious questions

We are not able to give personal religious rulings (fatwas). For guidance on Islamic law or your own situation, please speak to a qualified scholar or your local mosque. Our [disclaimer](/disclaimer) explains why.

This privacy policy explains what information Quran Universe ("we", "us") collects when you use quranuniverse.app (the "site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: you can read the whole site without an account, we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, we show ads through Google AdSense to support the site, and we never sell your data.

## Information we collect

### Information you do not have to give us

You do not need an account to use Quran Universe, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to read the site.

### Technical information from your visit

Like every website, our servers receive basic technical information when your browser requests a page. This includes your IP address, browser type, device type, the page you requested, the referring page and the date and time of the request. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes this information to deliver pages, protect the site from abuse and attacks, and keep it running. Cloudflare may keep short-term logs for these purposes under its own privacy policy.

We use this information only to operate, secure and improve the site. We do not use it to build profiles of individual visitors.

### Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix any issue you report.

## Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Quran Universe itself does not set cookies, but the third-party services described below (Google Analytics and Google AdSense) do, and Cloudflare may set security cookies.

The site stores one small setting in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme preference, so the site looks the way you chose on your next visit. This value never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies to tell real visitors apart from automated traffic. These are not used for advertising.

## Analytics and advertising

### Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are read most and how people arrive here. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited, time on site, approximate location (derived from your IP address), browser and device type. We use this information only in aggregate to improve the site. You can learn how Google uses this data at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and you can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads on the site. Ads help cover the cost of running Quran Universe and keep it free for everyone.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices) or, in the EU, [www.youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu).

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your browsing. For more on how Google uses advertising data, see [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads or analytics cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

We do not run social media pixels or session recording on the site.

### Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to understand how the site appears in Google search results. Search Console reports aggregated search data, such as which search terms showed our pages, and does not place any script or cookie on your device when you visit the site.

## Fonts and other resources

The fonts used on the site are bundled with it and served from our own domain, so loading a page does not send your information to a font provider.

## How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share information only:

- With service providers that help us run the site, such as our hosting and security provider, and only as needed for that service
- With Google, through the Google Analytics and Google AdSense cookies described above
- When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the site

## Data retention

Server and security logs are kept only as long as our providers need them for operation and security. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, after which it is deleted automatically. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with your message, and you can ask us to delete them.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how it is used. We do not hold analytics or advertising data in a form that identifies you; that data is processed by Google, and you can manage it through the opt-out links above. The only personal information we hold directly is an email you have sent us. To make a request, email [mubarakmmm5@gmail.com](mailto:mubarakmmm5@gmail.com).

## Children's privacy

Quran Universe is suitable for readers of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted on the site.

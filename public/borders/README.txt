Map layers for the Quran Universe timeline, generated by tools/cliopatria.mjs.
b<n>.json, index.json: historical polities from Cliopatria, part of the Seshat Global History Databank,
  https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.13363121).
  Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Changes: simplified, split into time bands,
  converted to TopoJSON, and tagged with the timeline's own state ids and ruling faiths.
  Borders are approximate and contested; the dataset authors ask readers to verify against other sources.
land.json: coastline and major rivers from Natural Earth 1:50m, public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com

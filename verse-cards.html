94:5AL-INSHIRAH

Ease is not after hardship. It is inside it.

Makkah. Rejection, mockery, few followers. Allah did not say "it will end." He said: look inside it, ease is already there, twice.

Months of job hunting. A sick parent. You keep saying "when this is over." Do not wait for over. Look for the ease that is here already.

Hardship, then relief. Two seasons.

One season, both at once. Find the second one.

Name one ease you have right now, inside your current difficulty. Write it down.

12:25YUSUF

He ran. She grabbed. The door opened on both of them, and she spoke first.

A slave in a minister's house in Egypt. Doors locked, the wife pressing him. He chose the door, got caught at it anyway. She spoke first, as the victim.

You did the right thing and it still blew up. The person who pushed you now tells the manager, the family, the group chat, that you were the problem.

Doing right protects me from being accused.

Doing right leaves evidence. Truth was on him while the lie was being told.

Caught in a false version of events? State what happened once, plainly. Then let the torn shirt speak.

2:261AL-BAQARAH

You give one. Allah grows it to seven hundred. And can grow it more.

New community in Madinah, poor, building a mosque, feeding migrants. Giving felt like losing. Allah showed them farming instead of subtracting.

Salary just hit. Rent, bills, a bit left. The donation link is open. Your brain says "minus 50." The verse says "times 700."

Giving is subtraction from my account.

Giving is planting. The seed leaves my hand so it can grow.

Give one small amount today. Picture seven ears.

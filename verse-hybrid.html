Al-Inshirah · 94:5Makkah

Ease is not after hardship. It is inside it.

Where relief actually lives

The one word that changes it
afterhardship, then one day, ease
the verse says
withma'a: one hardship, two eases, same season

Makkah. Rejection, mockery, few followers. Allah did not say "it will end." He said: look inside it, ease is already there, and it is there twice.

Months of job hunting. A sick parent. You keep saying "when this is over." The verse says: do not wait for over. Find the ease that is here already.

Hardship, then relief. Two seasons.

One season, both at once. Find the second one.

Try this

Name one ease you have right now, inside your current difficulty. Write it down.

Yusuf · 12:25Makkah

He ran. She grabbed. She spoke first.

Doing right leaves evidence, even while the lie is being told

Two versions, one shirt
her story"he wanted evil with your wife"spoken first
torn from the back= he was leaving, not comingthe evidence

A slave in a minister's house in Egypt. Doors locked, the wife pressing him. He chose the door and got caught at it anyway. She spoke first, as the victim.

You did the right thing and it still blew up. The person who pushed you now tells the manager, the family, the group chat, that you were the problem. Truth was on him while the lie was being told.

Doing right protects me from being accused.

Doing right leaves evidence. The shirt was torn from the back.

Try this

Caught in a false version of events? State what happened once, plainly. Then let the torn shirt speak.

Al-Baqarah · 2:261Madinah

You give one. Allah grows it to seven hundred.

How spending for Allah is counted

Two ways to count it
minus 50subtraction from my account
the verse says
times 700planted, not spent, and more for whom He wills

New community in Madinah, poor, building a mosque, feeding migrants. Giving felt like losing. Allah showed them farming instead of subtracting.

Salary just hit. Rent, bills, a bit left. The donation link is open. Your brain says "minus 50." The verse says "times 700."

Giving is subtraction from my account.

Giving is planting. The seed leaves my hand so it can grow.

Try this

Give one small amount today. Picture seven ears.
